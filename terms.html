Terms of Service & Deposit Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Last Updated: October 29, 2026

Thank you for choosing Diseños Luna for your milestone event stationery! We take immense pride in handcrafting immersive, interactive digital invitation websites for Quinceañeras, Weddings, Sweet 16s, and milestone celebrations.

These Terms of Service outline our design process, payment policies, turnaround expectations, and revision rules to ensure a seamless and joyful experience.

1 Custom Design Deposit Policy

Mandatory Deposit Requirement To ensure dedicated studio time and immediate scheduling, a deposit is required before custom design drafting begins. Work will commence promptly once your initial deposit is confirmed.

Because each invitation is individually customized with bespoke color matching, custom animated wax seals, interactive envelopes, and typography layouts, the deposit covers initial designer hours, staging setup, and personalized assets.

  • Deposit Amount: The required deposit is stated clearly on our pricing page and order form based on your selected package.
  • Balance Payment: The remaining balance is due after you review and approve your live interactive design proof, before final release and broad sharing.

2 Cancellation & Refund Policy

We strive for 100% client delight on every project. Because our work involves custom creative labor and non-recoverable design hours:

  • Before Work Begins: If you request a cancellation before any design work or draft setup has commenced, your deposit will be refunded in full.
  • After Work Begins: Once custom drafting, asset cutting, and interactive prototyping have begun, the deposit is non-refundable.
  • Final Publication: Once final proof approval is provided and the invitation link is fully published, payments are non-refundable.

3 Turnaround Times & Delivery

We pride ourselves on swift, reliable turnaround:

  • Initial Interactive Draft: Typically delivered within 24 to 48 business hours after receiving your complete event details, photos, and music selection.
  • Draft Delivery Method: You receive a private, interactive staging link via text, WhatsApp, or email to test the envelope opening, audio playback, RSVP form, and navigation buttons directly on your smartphone.

4 Revisions & Proofing Policy

We want your invitation to be absolute perfection. We provide:

  • Unlimited Minor Revisions: We happily fix spelling mistakes, update event times, swap out photos, update venue addresses, or refine wording at no extra charge during the proofing phase.
  • Major Scope Changes: A complete theme redesign, color palette overhaul, or structural restructuring requested after initial draft approval may incur an additional design revision fee.

5 1-Year Hosting & Digital Keepsake

12 Months of Active Online Hosting Included Your digital invitation website remains actively hosted, fully interactive, and accessible online for a minimum of 1 full year (12 months) following your celebration date.

This allows your custom web link to serve as an interactive digital keepsake that you, your family, and friends can revisit to relive the celebration. If you ever wish to take down the site earlier or request extended multi-year hosting, simply notify our team.
