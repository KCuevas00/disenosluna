Trust & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Last Updated: October 29, 2026

At Diseños Luna, we celebrate life's most cherished milestones—Quinceañeras, Weddings, Sweet 16s, and private celebrations. We recognize the deeply personal nature of event planning and treat your personal data, celebrant portraits, and guest information with utmost integrity, confidentiality, and security.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you order or visit our custom digital invitation websites, how it is used, and how we protect your family's privacy.

1 Information We Collect

To design and deliver your bespoke digital invitation website, we may collect:

  • Client Contact Details: Name, email address, phone number, and preferred contact platform (WhatsApp, Instagram, or Phone).
  • Event Information: Celebrant names, event dates, ceremony and reception venue names, GPS addresses, schedules, and custom parent/padrino dedications.
  • Event Media: Portrait photos, family photos, and audio soundtrack selections provided by you for inclusion in the invitation website.
  • Guest RSVP Responses: Guest names, headcount, phone numbers, attendance confirmation, and dietary/song notes submitted through the invitation's RSVP form.

2 How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely for the purpose of creating, personalizing, and hosting your event invitation:

  • To design, code, and launch your private interactive digital invitation website.
  • To provide you with design proofs, draft previews, and milestone updates.
  • To deliver real-time RSVP confirmations and guest headcounts directly to you.
  • To process deposits and payments through secure, certified payment gateways.
Our Commitment: We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information, photos, or guest contact lists to third-party advertisers or data brokers. Ever.

3 Guest RSVP Data Confidentiality

Guest RSVP confirmations submitted through your invitation website are collected exclusively for the event hosts. Guest phone numbers or emails collected during RSVP confirmation are used solely for attendance verification and important event reminders. They are never shared publicly or used for marketing.

4 Secure Cloud Media Storage & CDN

To ensure your invitation opens instantaneously on guests' smartphones anywhere in the world, photos and music provided for your website are hosted on secure enterprise cloud storage (Cloudflare R2 and Global Edge CDN) protected by SSL/TLS encryption.

5 Payment Security

All financial transactions (deposits and package balances) are conducted through certified, PCI-compliant payment processors such as Stripe, direct bank transfer (Zelle), or authorized debit/credit card gateways. Diseños Luna never collects, processes, or stores raw credit card numbers or banking passwords on our servers.

6 Keepsake Duration & Data Removal

Each digital invitation website remains actively hosted online for a minimum of 1 full year (12 months) following your celebration date, allowing it to serve as an interactive digital keepsake for your family.

If at any point after your event you wish to take down the invitation, remove personal portraits, or delete RSVP records, simply contact us and we will promptly remove the files from live hosting.

7 Third-Party Links & Map Services

Our invitations include interactive navigation buttons that launch Google Maps, Apple Maps, or Waze to help guests navigate to church and reception halls. These external services operate under their respective privacy policies.

8 Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to request data updates or removal, please reach out to us:

  • Business: Diseños Luna
  • Website: disenosluna.com
  • Online Contact: Contact Form & Speed Dial
